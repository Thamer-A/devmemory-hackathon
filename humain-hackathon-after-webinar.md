## 2. Format and rules

- **Individuals only.** There are no teams.
- Pick **one of the three challenges**. Do not combine them or work on all three.
- The "What Your Solution May Solve" scenarios are **examples, not requirements**. You can define your own problem or use case if it meets the challenge's core objective and shows the required capabilities.
- **No presentation in a meeting.** Judges review your submitted project page and deliverables. There is no live demo or live pitch.
- Projects will **not** be published or shared outside the hackathon.

## 3. Deliverables

Submit everything through the platform by the deadline.

1. **Presentation:** PowerPoint (PPT) format, **8 to 10 slides**. You can use more, but 8 to 10 is the target.
   - There is no mandatory template. Make your own.
2. **Demo video:** one recorded video of **3 minutes or less**.
   - It is a separate deliverable from the presentation.
   - It should show the solution working, not repeat the slides.
   - A live demo is not required.
3. **Source code**, either uploaded or as a GitHub link.
   - A public GitHub repo is allowed, and you can link it on your project page.
   - You must still upload the required deliverables on the platform itself. The link does not replace them.
4. **Project page** on the platform.
5. **Test results and methodology**, covering the unseen or held-out testing you ran (see section 5). Include these in the submission.

### Suggested slide outline (optional, can be reorganized)

1. Project Overview: name, selected challenge, short intro
2. Problem Statement: the specific problem and why it matters
3. Proposed Solution: your approach and how it solves the problem
4. Technical Approach: architecture, AI models, tools, technologies
5. Implementation: what you built, main features, how it works
6. Testing & Results: methodology, performance, validation
7. Innovation & Value: what makes it unique, potential impact
8. Challenges & Future Improvements: limitations and next steps
9. Conclusion & Demo: summary and a link or demo of the prototype

The organizers stress showing *how* the solution works and giving *evidence* of what you achieved, not only what the problem is.

### Suggested demo video outline (3 minutes max)

1. Introduction: the project and the problem
2. Solution walkthrough: main features
3. Demonstration: a realistic use case, step by step
4. AI in action: how it processes information, decides, and produces outputs or actions
5. Results: outcomes and how well it addresses the challenge

## 4. Judging criteria

Submissions are scored on six criteria.

1. **Task Performance:** objective performance on unseen or held-out test cases.
2. **Robustness & Adaptation:** staying effective when conditions, inputs, or requirements change.
3. **Evaluation & Iteration:** systematic testing, reproducible improvements, regression checks, and validation that key components contribute.
4. **Technical Judgment & Creativity:** quality of technical and design decisions, trade-offs, and meaningful value over simpler alternatives.
5. **Engineering Quality:** reproducible execution, clear and inspectable implementation, reliable performance, maintainable code.
6. **Communication:** clarity in explaining the problem, solution, methodology, results, and key technical decisions.

## 5. Testing, data, and models

- **You define and run your own testing.** That includes held-out or unseen cases. Report the methodology and results in your submission.
  - Judges evaluate from your submitted evidence and results, including performance across different scenarios. They do not run hidden tests on their side.
  - Show robustness and the ability to handle cases beyond your initial tests.
- **Data:** you may use whatever data you want. No dataset is being provided.
- **Synthetic data is allowed.** Explain clearly how you generated it and how you used it for testing and validation.
- **Input format:** there is no required format. Define what suits your solution and show it working in the prototype.
- **No API credits, API keys, or HUMAIN models (including ALLaM) will be provided.**
  - Use publicly available models, APIs, and tools, or whatever you already have access to.
  - You are free to choose your own models and technologies.
