### Challenge Direction

System recovery agent defined (43:06)
- Challenge 1: Agentic Recovery Of A Failing System
- Agent should identify, diagnose, and help recover failed systems or code
- Expected capabilities include analyzing logs and system metrics, identifying likely causes, and recommending corrective actions
- Participants may use available tools, coding systems, AI tools, and external resources

Efficient AI delivery prioritized (44:46)
- Challenge 2: Deliver AI With Less Compute
- Solutions should deliver high-quality AI results with fewer resources
- Possible approaches include model selection, prompt simplification, reduced processing, and efficient result checking
- Participants retain freedom to choose models, tools, and technical approaches

Autonomous business process targeted (46:29)
- Challenge 3: Autonomous Enterprise
- Participants identify one business process that can benefit from AI and autonomy
- Solutions should use available information and deliver results with minimal human intervention
- Potential applications include engineering workflows, sales processes, and cross-team handoffs

Narrowed challenge focus encouraged (56:13)
- Broad challenges are intended to support creativity and innovation
- Participants may focus on a specific process, department, or business context
- Existing projects may serve as starting points if adapted to the selected challenge
- Challenge details, example solutions, and judging criteria scheduled for platform publication
